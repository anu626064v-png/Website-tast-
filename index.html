SINCE 1992

A Legacy of
Sweet Excellence

Mithai Mahal was born from a simple yet profound belief — that the finest sweets should be an experience, not just a taste.

Founded by Master Halwai Shri Brijmohan Sharma in the pink city of Jaipur, we have spent over three decades perfecting the art of traditional mithai making using recipes passed down through five generations.

Today, under the guidance of his son, we continue to blend heritage with contemporary elegance — sourcing the finest pistachios from Iran, saffron from Kashmir, and almonds from California, all crafted in small batches with pure A2 desi ghee.
